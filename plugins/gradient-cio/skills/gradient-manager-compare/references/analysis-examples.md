# Analysis examples

Fictional examples marked **(TEST)** show the required depth. Use the pattern, never these values.

## Side-by-side comparison

**Strong (TEST)**
> (TEST) Alder Ridge reports $8.2 billion of regulatory AUM and 41 clients, versus Brook Harbor’s $5.6 billion and 73 clients, while both name independent custodians [S3]. The comparison highlights different operating scale and client concentration, but Form ADV values are adviser-reported and the filings are four months apart.

**Weak contrast:** “Alder Ridge is larger and Brook Harbor has more clients [S3].” It lacks analytical significance and filing-basis limitations.

## Operational flags

**Strong (TEST)**
> (TEST) Brook Harbor has two Medium provider-change flags and one unresolved auditor confirmation, while Alder Ridge has one Low filing-freshness flag [S5]. The evidence separates verification work by manager without ranking either firm, although one Brook Harbor fund could not be resolved and its provider record remains unavailable.

**Weak contrast:** “Brook Harbor has more operational concerns [S5].” It omits severity, the unresolved record, and the non-ranking purpose.

## Form 13F overlap

**Strong (TEST)**
> (TEST) The managers share 18 reported issuers, equal to 42% of Alder Ridge’s and 29% of Brook Harbor’s disclosed equity value [S7]. Overlap is asymmetric and relevant only to the reported long books because Form 13F is lagged and excludes shorts, derivatives, cash, private assets, and most non-US securities.

**Weak contrast:** “The managers have significant portfolio overlap [S7].” It gives no denominator and overstates incomplete firm-level filing evidence.

## ADV–13F consistency and events

**Strong (TEST)**
> (TEST) Alder Ridge’s ADV lists discretionary equity activity, consistent with its 13F filing, while Brook Harbor filed a new ownership amendment 23 days after its latest 13F quarter end [S9]. The timing creates a review point rather than a contradiction, because the forms cover different dates and reporting scopes.

**Weak contrast:** “The filings are mostly consistent [S9].” It fails to identify manager-specific evidence, event timing, or scope differences.
