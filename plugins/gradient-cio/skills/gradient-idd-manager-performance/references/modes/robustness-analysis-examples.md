# Robustness analysis examples

## Window sensitivity

**Strong example (TEST):** Observation: Annualized return ranged from 5.9% to 7.8% across four governed windows [S3]. Why it matters: The conclusion varies with the selected start date. Uncertainty: Missing pre-2020 history limits cycle coverage. What would change the view: A governed 2015-start result below 3.0%.

## Rolling evidence

**Strong example (TEST):** Observation: Eight of twelve returned rolling periods were positive [S4]. Why it matters: Positive outcomes occurred more often than negative ones. Uncertainty: Twelve periods provide limited persistence evidence. What would change the view: Twelve additional periods with at least eight negative results.

## Regime evidence

**Strong example (TEST):** Observation: Results were positive in two of three returned regime slices [S5]. Why it matters: Performance was not uniformly resilient across regimes. Uncertainty: One regime contains only nine observations. What would change the view: At least 24 observations in every governed slice.
