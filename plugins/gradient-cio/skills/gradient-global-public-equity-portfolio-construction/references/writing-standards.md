# Writing Standards — Global Public Equity Construction

## Decision language

- Lead with `We recommend <sleeve, manager or allocation action>, subject to <conditions>`.
- State a separate `Committee action requested`.
- Recommendations concern portfolio structure, manager roles, funding and implementation—not individual
  security ratings.
- Never say a committee decision has been made before approval.

## Evidence discipline

- Every number carries `[S#]`; scaling and rounding are allowed, unsupported derivation is not.
- Preserve returned status, basis, units, period, currency, coverage and missing reason.
- Write `Not available — <reason>` for absent evidence.
- Separate historical results, governed status and forward assumptions.

## Public-equity commentary

- Historical attribution comes only from governed portfolio attribution.
- Do not infer active share, tracking error, style, factor tilt, region or market-cap exposure.
- A 13F row is lagged manager-reported look-through evidence, not proof that the client portfolio holds the
  issuer directly.
- Include the complete 13F lag, long-only, omitted-asset and FX caveat wherever look-through appears.
- Do not issue `buy`, `sell`, `hold`, `overweight`, `underweight`, fair-value or price-target opinions for an
  issuer.
- Forward values are assumptions, not forecasts or guarantees.

## Recommendation test

For each proposed action include:

1. sourced observation;
2. mandate and benchmark implication;
3. uncertainty or coverage limitation;
4. proposed portfolio-level action and timing;
5. approval condition and monitoring trigger.

Avoid unsupported labels such as `high conviction`, `best manager`, `persistent alpha` or `cheap`. Do not use
`simulated attribution`, legacy local-calculation tags or uncited local calculations.
