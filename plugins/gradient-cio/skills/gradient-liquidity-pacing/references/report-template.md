# Liquidity and pacing report template

Use this exact order:

1. Executive Liquidity Assessment
2. Policy Minimums and Liquidity Tiers
3. Constrained, Suspended and Queued NAV
4. Unfunded Commitments
5. Commitment Pacing Projection
6. Stressed-Regime Liquidity
7. Capital-Call Coverage
8. Risks and Follow-ups
9. Coverage
10. Appendix — Sources and methods

Keep Section 5 when pacing is unavailable and use the exact typed unavailable
message. The executive assessment may describe coverage pressure but does not
authorize a commitment or pacing plan.
