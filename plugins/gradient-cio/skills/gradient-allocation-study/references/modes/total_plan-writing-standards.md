# Total-plan writing standards

- Call the report an allocation study, not an optimization or implemented
  rebalance.
- Keep current actual, policy target, and candidate weights distinct.
- Label every expected-return and Sharpe figure with source and basis.
- Describe Euler contribution as risk contribution, never return attribution.
- State regime and stress results as CMA scenarios, not forecasts.
- Use **Mapped by fallback** for `asset_classification_fallback`; do not call it
  look-through or security-level mapping.
- When pacing cash flows are missing, retain the liquidity sections and use
  **Not available — commitment cash flows not loaded** for pacing.
