# Total-plan allocation study template

Use this exact order:

1. Executive Decision
2. Mandate and Policy Basis
3. Current, Policy and Candidate Mixes
4. Expected Return Assumptions
5. CMA Risk and Euler Contribution
6. Regime and Stress Scenarios
7. Liquidity and Capital-Call Coverage
8. Commitments and Pacing
9. Implementation Constraints
10. Risks, Open Items and Approvals
11. Coverage
12. Appendix — Sources and methods
13. Appendix B — Server Metric Methods and Disclosures

Section 3 shows current actual, policy target, and candidate weights. Section 4
shows `user × policy_targets` and `cma_implied × policy_targets` side by side by
sleeve with a signed bp gap and one-line explanation. Section 5 uses only the
returned CMA portfolio-analytics risk and Euler contribution. Section 11
includes **Mapped by fallback**, unmapped share, covered share, and the returned
mapping basis.
