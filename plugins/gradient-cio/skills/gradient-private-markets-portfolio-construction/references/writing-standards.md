# Writing Standards — Private Markets Construction

## Decision language

- Lead with `We recommend <action>, subject to <conditions>` and a separate `Committee action requested`.
- State sizing, timing and funding source only when cited.
- Distinguish recommendation, committee approval, implementation and later monitoring.
- Never say a decision has been made before approval.

## Evidence discipline

- Every number carries `[S#]`; display scaling and rounding are allowed, unsupported derivation is not.
- Preserve returned status, basis, units, as-of date, coverage and missing reason.
- Write `Not available — <reason>`, never zero or “none,” for absent evidence.
- Forward values are assumptions, not forecasts or guarantees.

## Private-market commentary

- State valuation dates and lags near every NAV-based interpretation.
- Keep NAV, commitment and unfunded amounts distinct.
- Discuss unfunded coverage and near-term calls before return expectations.
- A pacing chart is a returned projection under its stated assumptions, not a promise of calls or
  distributions.
- Do not infer vintage, strategy, geography or manager diversification from labels that the evidence does
  not provide.
- Diligence evidence supports conditions and follow-up questions, not claims of manager quality or skill.

## Recommendation test

For each proposed action include:

1. sourced observation;
2. why it matters to the mandate;
3. uncertainty or coverage limitation;
4. proposed action and timing;
5. approval condition or monitoring trigger.

Avoid `clearly`, `obviously`, `guaranteed`, `safe`, `best-in-class`, `top quartile` and unsupported
superlatives. Do not use `simulated attribution`, legacy local-calculation tags or uncited local calculations.
