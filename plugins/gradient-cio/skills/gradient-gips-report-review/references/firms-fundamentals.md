# 2020 GIPS Standards for Firms — Sections 1–3

Source: CFA Institute, *2020 GIPS Standards for Firms* (gipsstandards.org). Provision numbers are from that
document. Wording in quotes is verbatim.

## Section 1 — Fundamentals of Compliance (requirements)

| # | Requirement | What to check |
|---|-------------|---------------|
| 1.A.1 | "The GIPS standards must be applied on a firm-wide basis." | The claim covers the whole firm, not one product or desk |
| 1.A.2 | "The firm must be defined as an investment firm, subsidiary, or division held out to the public as a distinct business entity." | The firm definition is disclosed (4.C.3) and matches how the firm markets itself |
| 1.A.3 | At least 5 years of compliant performance (or since inception if shorter), then one more year each year until there are at least 10 | Track record length in the GIPS Report |
| 1.A.4 | Comply with all applicable requirements, including Guidance Statements | Recent Guidance Statements applied (OCIO, fiduciary management, etc.) |
| 1.A.5 | Document policies and procedures, apply them consistently, and monitor changes to the standards | Ask for the P&P manual and its last update date |
| 1.A.6 | Comply with applicable laws and regulations | Conflicts with law disclosed (4.C.27) |
| 1.A.7 | "The firm must not present performance or performance-related information that is false or misleading." | Marketing figures match the GIPS Report |
| 1.A.8 | Must not claim to be "in compliance … except for" | Search marketing, DDQ and ADV for partial claims |
| 1.A.9 | "In accordance", "in compliance" or "consistent" wording, or similar, is prohibited (other than the required compliance statement) | Watch for "GIPS-consistent", "calculated per GIPS", "follows GIPS methodology" |
| 1.A.10 | Reports to existing clients may refer to GIPS calculation methods only if the firm is compliant | Client reports from non-compliant firms |
| 1.A.11–1.A.17 | Give GIPS Reports to prospective clients and to prospective investors in limited distribution pooled funds; update annually within 12 months of the period end; be able to show reasonable efforts | Report date vs latest year-end; ask how distribution is tracked |
| 1.A.18–1.A.19 | Benchmarks must reflect the investment mandate, objective or strategy; price-only benchmarks are prohibited | Benchmark fit; total-return benchmark |
| 1.A.20–1.A.21 | Material errors must be corrected and the corrected report given to the verifier, current clients and past recipients of the report | Ask for error-correction history |
| 1.A.27 | "The firm must not link actual performance to historical theoretical performance." | Backtested or model history linked to live returns |
| 1.A.28 | "Changes in the firm's organization must not lead to alteration of historical performance." | Restated history after M&A or reorganization |
| 1.A.29–1.A.30 | Non-compliant performance (TWR or MWR) must not be linked to compliant performance for periods after the minimum effective compliance date | Periods of non-compliance disclosed (4.C.20) |
| 1.A.35 | Time-weighted returns are required unless the firm controls external cash flows AND the portfolio is closed-end, fixed life, fixed commitment, or invests mostly in illiquid investments | MWR use is justified |
| 1.A.36 | Choose TWR, MWR or both, and apply the choice consistently | Return type changes disclosed (4.C.42) |
| 1.A.38 | File the GIPS Compliance Notification Form with CFA Institute initially and then annually by **30 June** | Ask for confirmation of the latest filing |

**Portability (performance from a past firm or affiliation).** Linking a past track record is optional. When
history is ported, the decision-makers and investment process must remain substantially intact, and the
records supporting the history must be retained. Disclose it (4.C.37). An acquired non-compliant business
must be brought into compliance within one year, prospectively.

## Section 2 — Input Data and Calculation Methodology (requirements)

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 2.A.1 | Total firm assets = all discretionary and non-discretionary assets the firm manages. Exclude advisory-only assets and uncalled committed capital |
| 2.A.2 | Total firm assets net of discretionary leverage; actual assets only |
| 2.A.3 | No double counting of assets |
| 2.A.4 | Composite and pooled fund performance uses only actual assets managed by the firm |
| 2.A.8 | Total returns |
| 2.A.9 | Trade-date accounting |
| 2.A.10 | Accrual accounting for fixed income and other interest-bearing investments |
| 2.A.13 | Returns after transaction costs |
| 2.A.15 | Returns net of discretionary leverage |
| 2.A.19 | Valuations at fair value |
| 2.A.23–2.A.24 | TWR: value portfolios at least monthly (month-end) and on the date of any large cash flow; calculate returns at least monthly |
| 2.A.28 | MWR: value at least annually and at the end of the period; daily external cash flows |
| 2.A.35 | Composite TWRs calculated at least monthly |
| 2.A.36 | Composite returns asset-weighted, using beginning-of-period values or a method that reflects both beginning values and external cash flows, or the aggregate method |
| 2.A.37–2.A.38 | Gross-of-fees = after transaction costs; net-of-fees = after transaction costs and investment management fees (including performance fees and carried interest) |

## Section 3 — Composite and Pooled Fund Maintenance (requirements)

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 3.A.1 | Create composites for every strategy managed or offered as a segregated account |
| 3.A.2 | "All actual, fee-paying, discretionary segregated accounts must be included in at least one composite." |
| 3.A.3 | Fee-paying discretionary pooled funds must be in a composite if they meet a composite definition |
| 3.A.4 | Non-fee-paying portfolios may be included only if they follow the same policies |
| 3.A.5 | Composites defined by mandate, objective or strategy, and include all portfolios that meet the definition, regardless of legal structure |
| 3.A.6 | "Any change to a composite definition must not be applied retroactively." |
| 3.A.7 | New portfolios added on a timely, consistent, composite-specific basis |
| 3.A.8 | Only portfolios managed for the full measurement period |
| 3.A.9 | "Terminated portfolios must be included in the historical performance" through their last full period |
| 3.A.10 | Portfolios not moved between composites unless documented client-directed changes or a composite redefinition |
| 3.A.11 | Minimum asset levels applied consistently and prospectively |
| 3.A.12–3.A.13 | Significant cash flow removal and temporary new accounts defined ex ante, composite by composite |
| 3.A.14 | Wrap-fee portfolios included per policy |
| 3.A.15–3.A.18 | Carve-outs include cash and income, are representative of a standalone portfolio, and, if cash is allocated, cash is allocated to carve-outs from all portfolios; a separate standalone composite is required once standalone portfolios exist |
| 3.A.19 | "The firm must not combine different composites, pooled funds, or carve-outs to create a simulated strategy." |

**Diligence angle:** survivorship bias (3.A.9), cherry-picked accounts (3.A.2, 3.A.5), retroactive
redefinition (3.A.6) and carve-outs (3.A.15–3.A.18) are the most common ways a track record can be flattered.
Ask for the composite list (4.C.15) and the composite's history of entries and exits.
