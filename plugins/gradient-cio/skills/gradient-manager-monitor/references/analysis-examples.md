# Analysis examples

Fictional examples marked **(TEST)** show the required depth. Use the pattern, never these values.

## Changes and alerts

**Strong (TEST)**
> (TEST) Northstar Advisers filed an ownership amendment and received one new Medium custody alert during the seven-day window; its prior review is 11 months old [S3]. The co-occurrence makes evidence refresh time-sensitive, although the filing states the change but does not establish operational impact and fund-level custody resolution remains incomplete.

**Weak contrast:** “Northstar had changes and alerts this week [S3].” It lacks the named events, review age, monitoring significance, and coverage limit.

## Review calendar

**Strong (TEST)**
> (TEST) Three manager reviews are due within 30 days, including Harbor Credit in nine days with two open High findings older than 120 days [S5]. The calendar concentrates near-term diligence attention, but one due date is organization-entered and the latest governed review record has not confirmed it.

**Weak contrast:** “Several reviews are coming due [S5].” It omits count, timing, severity, aged findings, and the unconfirmed-date limitation.
