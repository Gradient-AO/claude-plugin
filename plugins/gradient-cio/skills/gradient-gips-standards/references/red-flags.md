# GIPS Red Flags in Manager Due Diligence

Rate each flag found using the scale in output-format.md. Severity below is the default; adjust with judgment.

## Claim and wording

| Red flag | Default severity | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| "GIPS compliant except for…", "substantially compliant" | High | 1.A.8 |
| "Calculated in accordance with GIPS", "GIPS-consistent", "follows GIPS methodology" by a firm that does not claim compliance | High | 1.A.9 |
| Compliance claimed for one strategy, fund or team rather than the firm | High | 1.A.1 |
| Compliance statement wording altered or missing the verification sentence | Medium | 4.C.1 |
| Claims verification but cannot produce a verification report, or the report names a different entity | High | 4.C.1, 4.C.3 |
| Not listed as a GIPS-claiming firm, or no confirmation of the 30 June notification filing | Medium | 1.A.38 |
| DDQ answer on GIPS contradicts the GIPS Report, marketing deck or Form ADV | High | 1.A.7 |

## Track record integrity

| Red flag | Default severity | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| Backtested or model performance linked to live performance | High | 1.A.27 |
| History restated after a reorganization or acquisition | High | 1.A.28 |
| Ported track record without disclosure, or key decision-makers no longer present | High | 4.C.37 |
| Composite redefined retroactively; benchmark changed retroactively without disclosure | High | 3.A.6, 4.C.32 |
| Terminated accounts missing (survivorship); account count drops while returns improve | High | 3.A.9 |
| Composite has very few portfolios relative to firm strategy assets (cherry-picking) | Medium | 3.A.2, 3.A.5 |
| Carve-outs with allocated cash, especially if not labeled | Medium | 3.A.15–3.A.18, 4.C.28 |
| Composite has five or fewer portfolios | Low (note for context) | 4.C.39 |
| Performance from a sub-advisor period not disclosed | Medium | 4.C.29 |
| Track record shorter than five years without "since inception" explanation | Medium | 1.A.3 |
| GIPS Report more than 12 months stale | Medium | 1.A.12 |

## Numbers and methodology

| Red flag | Default severity | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| Gross-only returns, or net returns using a model fee lower than the fee schedule offered | Medium | 4.C.7, 4.C.11 |
| Price-only benchmark, or benchmark not matching the mandate | Medium | 1.A.19, 1.A.18 |
| Composite assets exceed total firm assets, or firm assets include advisory-only/uncalled capital | High | 2.A.1, 2.A.3 |
| Missing 3-year standard deviation or dispersion without the required explanation | Low | 4.C.36, 4.C.40 |
| High % of Level 3 / unobservable-input assets without the 4.A.2 disclosure | Medium | 4.A.2 |
| Private fund MWR shown only with subscription-line effect | Medium | Section 7 |
| Material error corrections not disclosed or not redistributed | High | 1.A.20–1.A.21, 4.C.38 |

## Standard diligence request list (GIPS)

1. Latest GIPS Report for the composite or pooled fund under review, and the list of composite descriptions.
2. Latest verification report (and performance examination report, if any), with the management letter.
3. GIPS policies and procedures manual and its last revision date.
4. Composite membership history (portfolios added and removed, with reasons) for the full track record.
5. Error correction log for the past five years.
6. Reconciliation between the GIPS Report and the returns in the pitchbook, factsheet and DDQ.
7. For private funds: since-inception MWR with and without subscription lines, and the multiples.
