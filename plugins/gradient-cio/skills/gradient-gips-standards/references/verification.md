# Verification and Performance Examinations

Source: 2020 GIPS standards for firms (compliance statement provisions 4.C.1) and the GIPS Standards for
Verifiers. Verification is recommended, not required.

## What each one covers

| | Verification | Performance examination |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Whole firm (or asset owner) | One specific composite or pooled fund |
| Prerequisite | None | Firm must also be verified (usually done alongside) |
| What it tests | Whether policies and procedures for composite and pooled fund maintenance, and for calculating, presenting and distributing performance, are designed in compliance with GIPS and implemented firm-wide | Whether that composite's or fund's reports were prepared and presented in compliance, including the numbers |
| What it does **not** do | Give assurance that any specific composite's returns are accurate | Cover other composites |
| Required minimum period | At least one year, or since firm inception if less | Periods named in the report |

## Reading a verification report

Check each item and record it in the findings checklist:

1. **Verifier identity and independence.** An established GIPS verification firm (specialist performance
   firms or audit firms). Independence from the manager: no consulting on the same policies being verified,
   no performance-based fees.
2. **Periods covered.** Must match the periods named in the compliance statement. A gap between the
   verification period end and the latest annual period in the GIPS Report is a follow-up item; more than
   about 18 months is a red flag.
3. **Firm definition.** The entity named in the verification report matches the firm definition in the GIPS
   Report (4.C.3) and in the Form ADV.
4. **Opinion type.** An unqualified opinion is expected. Any qualification, emphasis of matter or scope
   limitation is a material finding.
5. **Performance examination.** If the manager says a composite was examined, confirm the composite name and
   periods in the examination report.
6. **Change of verifier.** Ask why and when; a recent change after a long relationship is a follow-up item.

## Questions to ask when the manager claims verification

- Provide the latest verification report and, if applicable, the performance examination report.
- Which periods are covered, and when is the next verification scheduled?
- Were there any findings or recommendations in the verifier's management letter?
- Has the firm made any material error corrections in the last five years?
- Confirm the latest GIPS Compliance Notification Form filing with CFA Institute (due 30 June each year).
