# 2020 GIPS Standards for Asset Owners (Sections 21–25)

Source: CFA Institute, *2020 GIPS Standards for Asset Owners* (gipsstandards.org). Asset owners are pension
funds, endowments, foundations, sovereign wealth funds and similar entities that manage investments on behalf of
participants, beneficiaries or the organization itself. Their GIPS Asset Owner Report goes to the **oversight
body** (board, investment committee, trustees), not to prospective clients.

Items marked † should be confirmed against the published text before citing as a deficiency.

## Fundamentals (21.A)

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 21.A.1 | Compliance must be met on an asset-owner-wide basis |
| 21.A.2 | Asset owner defined as the entity managing investments on behalf of participants, beneficiaries or itself |
| 21.A.3 | Must have discretion over total fund assets (directly or through hiring and firing external managers) |
| 21.A.4 | Initial compliance: at least **one year** of compliant performance (or since inception) — not five as for firms |
| 21.A.11 | Provide a GIPS Asset Owner Report for every total fund, and any additional composites it presents, to the oversight body |
| 21.A.13 | Update reports within 12 months of each annual period end |
| † | Same prohibitions as firms on partial-compliance and "in accordance with" wording, false or misleading information, and linking theoretical performance |
| † | Asset owners that compete for business (e.g., OCIO-like services to outside clients) may need to comply as a firm instead |

## Calculation (22.A)

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 22.A.5 | Total returns |
| 22.A.6 | Trade-date accounting |
| 22.A.10 | Returns after transaction costs |
| 22.A.12 | Returns net of leverage |
| 22.A.20–22.A.21 | TWR: value at least monthly and calculate at least monthly |
| 22.A.22–22.A.23 | MWR: value at least annually; daily external cash flows |
| † | Fair value; accrual accounting; external manager fees deducted in net returns |

## Total funds and composites (23.A)

| # | Requirement |
|---|-------------|
| 23.A.1 | A total fund includes all assets managed as part of the total fund's mandate |
| 23.A.4 | Any additional composites are defined by mandate, objective or strategy |

## TWR report (24.A required numbers)

| # | Item |
|---|------|
| 24.A.1.a | At least 1 year of performance (or since inception), building to at least 10 years |
| 24.A.1.b | **Net-of-fees returns are required for total funds** (net of external and internal management costs as defined) |
| 24.A.1.c | Total fund or composite return for each annual period |
| 24.A.1.f | Benchmark total return for each annual period |
| 24.A.1.g | Number of total funds or portfolios in the composite at each annual period end |
| 24.A.1.h–i | Total fund or composite assets, and total asset owner assets, at each annual period end |
| 24.A.1.j | 3-year annualized ex post standard deviation from monthly returns, when available |

## MWR report (25.A required numbers)

| # | Item |
|---|------|
| 25.A.1.a | Annualized since-inception MWR through the most recent annual period end |
| 25.A.1.d–g | Benchmark return, number of portfolios, composite assets and total asset owner assets |
| † | Paid-in capital, distributions, committed capital and TVPI/DPI/RVPI/PIC multiples where applicable |

## Required disclosures (24.C and 25.C)

- **24.C.1 Compliance statement:** "[Asset owner] has prepared and presented this report in compliance with the
  Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS®)." followed by either "[Asset owner] has been independently
  verified for the periods [dates]" (with the standard explanation of what verification covers) or "[Asset
  owner] has not been independently verified." † confirm exact wording.
- GIPS trademark notice.
- Total fund or composite description; benchmark description and key features (policy benchmark components,
  weights and rebalancing for custom/policy benchmarks).
- Reporting currency; inception dates.
- Which fees and costs are deducted in net returns.
- Use of external managers and the periods.
- Historical use of leverage, derivatives and short positions, if material.
- Significant events; changes to benchmarks and total fund definitions; material error corrections.
- Periods of non-compliance, if any.
- Policies for valuing investments, calculating performance and preparing reports are available on request.
