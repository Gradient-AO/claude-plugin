# GIPS Advertising Guidelines (Section 8) and the SEC Marketing Rule

Source: CFA Institute, *GIPS Advertising Guidelines Comparison for Firms* (gipsstandards.org) and the 2020
GIPS Standards for Firms, Section 8. SEC Rule 206(4)-1 summarized from the rule text (compliance date
4 November 2022).

## Three ways a compliant firm can market performance

1. Provide the GIPS Report with the material.
2. Follow the GIPS Advertising Guidelines (a "GIPS Advertisement").
3. Make no reference to GIPS at all (the performance then need not follow GIPS, but must not be misleading).

A piece that mentions GIPS without either a GIPS Report or the Advertising Guidelines elements is a finding.

## Required elements of a GIPS Advertisement

Advertising compliance statement (all types): **"[Firm] claims compliance with the Global Investment
Performance Standards (GIPS®)."** Plus the GIPS trademark notice, the reporting currency, how to obtain the GIPS
Report, and any conflict with laws or regulations.

| Element | Composite TWR | Composite MWR | Limited distribution pooled fund | Broad distribution pooled fund |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Periods | One of: 1-, 3- and 5-year annualized (+ since inception if under 5 years); period-to-date + 1/3/5-year; period-to-date + 5 annual returns; or annualized since-inception return | Since-inception MWR through the most recent period or year end (non-annualized if under 1 year) | TWR: same options as composite TWR. MWR: since-inception MWR | Same TWR options unless regulation sets periods, plus annualized since-inception return |
| Fee labeling | Gross-of-fees or net-of-fees | Gross or net | Gross or net of **total pooled fund fees** | Gross or net of total pooled fund fees; share-class or maximum-fee basis |
| Benchmark | Same as GIPS Report, TWR, same periods and currency | Same as GIPS Report, MWR | Same as GIPS Pooled Fund Report | Total return for same periods, with description (unless no appropriate benchmark) |
| Expense ratio | Not required | Not required | Current expense ratio, what it includes, whether performance fees excluded | Same, plus fund description and risk information |
| Where to get the report | How to obtain the GIPS Composite Report | Same | How to obtain the GIPS Pooled Fund Report | How to obtain performance information |

Supplemental information in an advertisement must be labeled and must not contradict the required numbers.

## SEC Marketing Rule (Rule 206(4)-1) — what to check for US-registered advisers

GIPS compliance does not satisfy the Marketing Rule on its own, and the rule does not require GIPS. Check
both where the manager is an SEC-registered adviser.

| Rule requirement | What to check |
|---|---|
| Gross performance only with net performance, equal prominence, same periods and method | Factsheets and pitchbooks showing gross only |
| 1-, 5- and 10-year periods (or life of the portfolio if shorter), ending on the most recent practicable date, equal prominence — **not required for private funds** | Separate account and composite marketing |
| No statement that the SEC approved or reviewed the performance | |
| Related performance must include all related portfolios (or exclusions that do not make it better) | Compare with the GIPS composite membership |
| Extracted performance must offer the total portfolio's performance | Carve-outs in marketing |
| Hypothetical performance (including model, backtested and target/projected returns) only with policies ensuring relevance to the audience, plus methodology and risk information | Ties to GIPS 1.A.27 (no linking theoretical to actual) and 4.C.48 |
| Predecessor performance: the people primarily responsible are still managing, the accounts are similar, all accounts included, and the history is disclosed | Ties to GIPS portability (4.C.37) |

**Common conflicts between the two regimes:** a GIPS Report showing gross returns only (allowed under GIPS
when net is not required) can fail the Marketing Rule's net requirement; GIPS Advertisement period options do
not all meet the rule's 1/5/10-year requirement; private fund presentations under GIPS Section 7 still need
Marketing Rule net-of-fee treatment.
